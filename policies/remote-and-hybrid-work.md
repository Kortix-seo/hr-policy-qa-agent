# Remote and hybrid work

This file is example policy text for the reference project in this repository: an open-source HR policy helpdesk agent that runs on Kortix, the open-source AI Operating System. The offices, distances and amounts below belong to an invented company, and a company adopting this repository replaces them. They are here to show the level of detail an answer needs before it can be quoted to an employee without a follow-up question. Nothing in this file is Kortix's own remote-work policy. The repository is open source, so the shape can be copied and the numbers replaced.

## Hybrid baseline

Anyone who lives within 50 miles of a company office comes in three days a week: Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Monday and Friday are remote by default. A team that needs a different pattern writes it in the team handbook, and the team's director and HR approve the pattern once for the whole team rather than person by person.

## Working from another timezone

An employee can work from another timezone for up to 10 working days in a calendar year without approval. Two weeks counts as 10 working days, so a two-week stay inside the country needs notice but no approval. Tell your manager and HR at least 10 working days before the first day, name the timezone and the address where you will work, and keep at least four hours of overlap with the team's core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 in the team's home timezone.

More than 10 working days, a stay outside the country, or a move that changes your tax residence is not covered by this section. Those requests go to HR before anything is booked.

## Remote days

On a remote day, be reachable in the team's channel and join meetings on video. A remote day is not a substitute for leave. If you are unwell on a remote day, the sick leave section of the time off policy applies and your manager is told the same morning.

## Home office stipend

The home office stipend is $500, paid once per employee and never renewed. It covers a desk, a chair, a lamp, a webcam or another item for a home workspace. Claim it through the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase, attach itemized receipts, and mark the claim HOME-OFFICE. A single item that costs more than $500 sits outside the stipend. A manager can approve it, and the part above $500 is not reimbursed.

## Equipment stipend

The equipment budget is separate from the home office stipend: $1,200 every three years per person, spent on the laptop, monitor and peripherals described in `policies/learning-and-equipment.md`. The $500 stipend does not reduce it, and the $1,200 budget cannot be spent on furniture.

## Relocation

A move to another country, or to a city more than 50 miles from your current office, is a relocation. Ask HR in writing at least 60 days before the move, with the destination, the date and the reason. HR reviews the request with Finance and answers within 10 working days. Any support is agreed in that written answer and depends on the role's contract. This section describes the process; it does not promise a payment.

## What this file does not answer

This file does not decide whether one person's circumstances justify an arrangement outside these rules, and it does not settle tax, immigration or right-to-work questions raised by a move. Those go to an HR person through `escalation_rules.yaml`, with the question and the rule id attached, and no draft comes back.
