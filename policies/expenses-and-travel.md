# Expenses and travel

This file is example policy text for the reference project in this repository: an open-source HR policy helpdesk agent that runs on Kortix, the open-source AI Operating System. The limits and the per diem below belong to an invented company. They are written the way a policy has to be written before an agent can answer from it: one limit per line, a deadline for every claim, and no sentence that leaves the amount to interpretation. This is not Kortix's expense policy. The file is open source, so the form is yours to copy even though the numbers are not real.

## Receipts and the 14 day rule

Every expense above $25 needs an itemized receipt. Submit the claim within 14 days of the purchase, or within 14 days of the last day of a trip for anything bought on the trip. A claim with no receipt is returned unsent. Waiving the receipt requirement is an exception, and this policy does not grant one.

## Approvals and spend limits

Spend up to $500 needs no approval in advance, though the receipt rule still applies. Spend above $500 needs your manager's written approval before the purchase. A conference has its own ceiling: $2,500 for registration, travel and hotel together, approved before anything is booked. Rail is booked in standard class. A claim above any of these limits is an exception request, not an expense the policy allows.

## Per diem

The per diem is $60 a day for meals and incidentals on any trip with an overnight stay. The per diem replaces meal receipts, so a meal claimed at the per diem is not claimed again as an expense. A meal paid for by a customer, or included in a conference fee, is not claimed at all.

## Booking travel for a customer visit

1. Get written approval from your manager with the customer, the purpose, the dates and an estimate of the total cost.
2. Book through the travel tool. Use the corporate card only when the tool has no option for the route.
3. Book at least 14 days ahead whenever the trip allows it.

Flights are economy for a scheduled flight under six hours and premium economy for anything longer. Hotels are booked up to $220 a night before tax in a listed city and $160 elsewhere; the list of cities is in the travel tool. A customer visit follows the same three steps as any other trip, and the customer's name goes in the approval so that the cost has a reason attached to it.

## Corporate card

The corporate card is for travel and approved purchases only. Personal spend never goes on it. Reconcile the statement within 14 days, attaching the receipt for each line. A card line with no receipt is treated as an unclaimed expense and is recovered from the next payroll run.

## Reimbursement

An approved claim is paid with the next payroll run and no later than 30 days after approval. A claim in a foreign currency is converted at the rate the card or the bank applied on the date of the purchase, and the conversion line is part of the claim.

## What this file does not answer

This file does not waive a receipt, raise a limit or rule on one person's claim. A request to bend any of the numbers above goes to a person through `escalation_rules.yaml` under the exception rule, and no draft is written for it.
