# Learning budget and equipment

This file is example policy text for the reference project in this repository: an open-source HR policy helpdesk agent that runs on Kortix, the open-source AI Operating System. The budget, the laptop list and the three-year cycle below belong to an invented company. A company that adopts this repository replaces the amounts and keeps the structure, because the structure is what makes the file answerable. Nothing here is Kortix's own learning or equipment policy. The repository is open source, so a team can fork it and change the amounts.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of $1,000 a calendar year for courses, certifications, books and any conference that is not already covered as travel. The budget resets on 1 January and does not carry over. A certification that costs more than the budget you have left needs your manager's written approval before you book, and that approval states how much of the excess the team covers.

## Approval before you book

Ask your manager in writing with the course name, the provider, the cost and the dates. A reply arrives within five working days. Training that runs during working hours counts as working time. Training of up to two consecutive days needs no leave. Anything longer is booked as leave for the days beyond the second.

## Claiming a course you already paid for

If you paid for a course yourself, submit the receipt and the certificate of completion within 30 days of the course ending, through the expense tool, marked LEARNING. Reimbursement arrives with the next payroll run. The annual limit still applies: a course that started before it was approved is reimbursed only up to the budget that was unspent on the day it started.

## The laptop you get when you join

IT orders a 14-inch laptop from the standard catalog before your start date, so it is on your desk, or delivered to your home address, in your first week. The standard set is that laptop, one monitor up to 27 inches, a keyboard and a mouse. A different model is available only when the catalog model cannot run the software the role needs, and IT records the reason on the order.

## Equipment budget and the three-year cycle

Equipment spend is capped at $1,200 every three years per person, counted from the date of the last order. The cap covers the laptop, the monitor and the peripherals together. It is separate from the $500 home office stipend in `policies/remote-and-hybrid-work.md`, and it cannot be spent on furniture.

## Replacements inside three years

A replacement before the three years are up needs either a repair report from IT or your manager's approval. Report accidental damage in the same week it happens. IT collects the equipment on your last working day, and a laptop kept past that date is recovered at its depreciated value.

## What this file does not answer

This file does not rule on a request to exceed the learning budget, the equipment cap or the three-year cycle, and it does not compare one person's request with another's. Requests outside these numbers go to an HR person through `escalation_rules.yaml`, with no draft returned.
